## FAQ

**Q: Can I showcase public repos from organizations I belong to on my GitHub profile?**

Yes! There are two easy ways to do this:

1. **Pin the repository** — Go to your GitHub profile, click "Customize your pins", and you can pin up to six repos including ones from organizations you're a member of (as long as you have contributed to them).
2. **Link them in your README** — Add a direct link to the organization repo in this README, just like the projects listed above. This is a great way to highlight collaborative work even if the repo lives under an org account rather than your personal account.

Organization repos pinned to your profile and linked here will be visible to anyone who views your GitHub profile.
